# `liwan_products` implementation plan

> Step 2 of [the customers and products foundation design](../specs/2026-11-11-customers-and-products-foundation-design.md), which the owner approved on 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12 and named on 2026-11-13. Status: the owner decided the six owner points of section 4 on 2026-11-13 and added two decisions: one tax mode for every application, in the company settings, and finance invoice lines that carry a product. Later the same day the owner settled how that tax mode works (D5, D6, D21, D22): stock Odoo's own company setting with its lock, a three-way choice, a default tax per product that can be changed, and excise left for later. Nothing in it is built. The facts in section 3 were read on `main` at `5f91e53f9` and in the Odoo 19 and OCA sources by one read-only pass of one session. An independent reviewer read the plan against the code at `9ba36bdaa` on 2026-11-13 and reported 5 blocking, 14 medium and 10 minor findings; it read code and ran nothing. This revision answers them, and the owner decided the four points they raised the same day (D5, D23, D25). One probe was run, on a throwaway database with stock `sale` and `l10n_sa` only, for the tax facts of section 3; no LiwanOS test was run. The work changes schema and authorization across layers, so every pull request takes the full path: an implementer per layer, a tester, and a reviewer separate from both.

## 1. Goal

One addon, `liwan_products`, is the only writer of a product's definition on `product.template`. CRM, customer messages and appointments stop creating and editing products on their own, a product defined once is offered by every application that sells it, every price a screen shows comes from one function, and a tenant shapes the service from its own settings.

## 2. Scope

In: one tax mode on the company for every application, the service with its settings and price function, the tax bridge `liwan_products_account`, the three writers moved onto it, the "Products and services" screen with categories, tenant-defined fields and variants, the transfer between the two sharing modes, the move of the Meta catalog out of the payments bridge, and a product on finance invoice lines.

Out, by the spec: orders (`liwan_orders`, with the store), pricelists, combo components, inventory and availability from stock, sector presets, and a group of legal companies in one tenant. Booking offers (one service offered several times, decision 15 of the spec) need their own appointments plan; this plan only moves the existing one-type-one-product link onto the service. The appointments screens that set a type's price are built later, with that plan.

`liwan_customers` is on `main` and is the template for the addon's shape: capabilities, groups, tenant settings, feature gate and tests.

## 3. Facts the plan rests on

Each task rechecks the facts it uses. "Read" means the code was opened; "inferred" means the first test of the task must confirm it.

Writers of a product today (read):

- CRM creates, edits and archives "packages" as `product.template` rows under `crm.settings.manage` (`liwan_crm/controllers/lists.py:303-341`). A new package is stamped with the current company, marked `liwan_crm_package` (`models/product_template.py:8`), and has its sale and purchase taxes cleared (`lists.py:317-322`). The right is an ACL row with read, write and create on every product for the settings manager (`security/ir.model.access.csv:10-11`). Four places read the marker: the list domain (`lists.py:80-84`), the sale route (`controllers/sale.py:23-27`), the import (`models/intake.py:212-217`) and the lead field's domain (`models/crm_lead.py:19`). Four places read `list_price` directly: the package serializer (`serializers/lists.py:34-37`), the discount (`models/crm_lead_flow.py:33-44`), the sale (`:68-76`) and the import (`models/intake.py:321-335`). A lead stores the package as a `product.template` and copies its `list_price` when it is set or won (`models/crm_lead.py:17-21`, `models/crm_lead_flow.py:68-76`); the discount is computed from that copy. CRM settings are one row per company (`models/crm_settings.py:140-144,203`).
- Customer messages creates a product from the catalog editor as goods, in the catalog's company, marked `liwan_inbox_catalog` (`liwan_inbox_payments/controllers/catalog_items.py:67-73`), and edits name, description, price and image of those only (`:166-170`, `controllers/parsing.py:135-152`). Its right is the same kind of ACL row for `inbox.catalog.manage` (`security/ir.model.access.csv:11-12`), narrowed by two record rules on the marker (`security/inbox_payments_rules.xml:23-38`). It also lists every saleable product of the company to pick from (`:121-143`). A write override on `product.template` and `product.product` resyncs the Meta items when a watched field changes (`models/product.py:5-35`); it is a reader of changes, not a writer.
- Appointments creates one service product per paying booking type and writes its price and name under `sudo()`, after its own capability check (`liwan_appointments_payments/models/resource_booking_type.py`, `_liwan_price_product` and the name sync in `write`; `tools/arch_documented_sudo.json:21`). The link is the OCA column `product.template.resource_booking_type_id` (`oca-19.0/sale-workflow/sale_resource_booking/models/product_template.py:10`) with a LiwanOS unique constraint (`liwan_appointments_payments/models/product_template.py:10-12`), and the OCA sale line reads the type from the product (`sale_resource_booking/models/sale_order_line.py`, `_sync_resource_bookings`). The price is set through `PATCH /api/v1/appointments/types/<id>/payment`, and read back as the product's `list_price`, described as before taxes (`serializers/booking_payment.py:21-31`, contract `packages/contracts/src/appointments/payments.ts`). A search of `apps/web/app` found no page that calls it.
- Payments adds refund rules to `product.template` (a policy, amounts and notice tiers) and writes them elevated for the application that sells the product (`liwan_payments/models/refund_policy.py:35,48-52,91-111`, `tools/arch_documented_sudo.json:193`). These are payments' own fields, not part of a product's definition.
- Finance does not use products: no file of `liwan_finance` outside its tests names `product.template`, `product.product` or `product_id`. An invoice line carries no product today.

Tax today (read):

- An online order line takes the product's taxes and then replaces them: none for a company that is not registered for VAT, otherwise the same taxes with the price included or added, by the company's tax mode (`liwan_payments/models/order_service.py:67-84`).
- The tax mode is a per-company row of `liwan.payments.settings` (`liwan_payments/models/payments_settings.py:51,74-77`), edited from the payments settings card (`apps/web/app/components/payments/OrderSettingsCard.vue`). A branch can hold a row of its own. VAT registration is a stored boolean on each company, a branch included, off by default (`liwan_core/models/res_company.py:34,145-152`); changing it needs `settings.company.vat.manage`, which only the system administrator role holds, and the profile route writes the session's company only (`:163-172,208-221`, `liwan_api/controllers/company.py`). `liwan_payments`, `liwan_inbox_payments` and `liwan_api` call the helper; `liwan_finance` never does.
- The Meta catalog computes its own price with its own option, `prices_include_tax` per catalog, from the product's taxes (`liwan_inbox_payments/models/catalog.py:35`, `models/catalog_item.py:181-200`). It does not read the tax mode. So one company has two answers to "does this price include tax". Both default to included (`payments_settings.py:20-21`). The item keeps only taxes owned by the catalog's own company (`catalog_item.py:198`), so a branch catalog finds no tax of its root.
- The copies of a tax that the order service makes were added so that "an order can charge as the company decides while the original tax stays as other documents use it" (`liwan_payments/models/account_tax.py:8-11`, commit `16323f7f6`). They exist to give online orders a tax mode of their own, which the owner's decision of one tax mode removes.
- A finance invoice line has a label, a quantity, a unit price, a discount and the taxes the accountant picks; it names no product (`liwan_finance/serializers/invoice.py:18,51-66,101-121`). Each tax says for itself whether its price includes it (`serializers/tax.py:34`).
- Finance gives a branch its own journals and expects its own taxes: a tax is created in the session's company and listed for the session's companies only (`liwan_finance/controllers/finance_taxes.py:24-25,96`, `models/res_company.py:36-61`), and the fence is widened to a branch's ancestors for reading accounts alone (`models/access_service.py:83-96`). The root's taxes are hidden from a branch session; CRM's tax clearing exists because of it (`liwan_crm/controllers/lists.py:317-318`).

Stock tax mode (read in Odoo 19, and run on a throwaway database with `sale` and `l10n_sa` on 2026-11-13):

- `res.company.account_price_include` is `tax_included` or `tax_excluded`, default `tax_excluded` (`addons/account/models/company.py:272-278`). A tax follows it unless the tax carries `price_include_override` (`models/account_tax.py:142-147,322-329`). Run: a product at 115 with the 15% tax gives 115 + 17.25 = 132.25 under `tax_excluded` and 100 + 15 = 115 under `tax_included`, on a sale order line and on a manually typed invoice line alike.
- The setting cannot change once the company or a branch has any journal item, a draft invoice included (`company.py:325-328,992-995`). Run: the change is refused after one draft invoice. Run: a draft order made before a change kept 132.25 until it was recomputed, then showed 115, which is what the lock prevents.
- A tax follows the setting of the company that owns it (`account_tax.py:141`), and stock does not copy the setting from a root to its branches (`company.py:311-317`). Run: an order of a child company with a tax the root owns followed the root's setting, and changing the branch's own value changed nothing. Read, not run: a tax a branch owns follows the branch's own value, which is `tax_excluded` unless something sets it. Stock picks a product's taxes for a company by climbing from it to the first ancestor that owns one (`account_tax.py:5093-5102`).
- A tax with an explicit override ignores the company. Run: a copy of the 15% tax set to `tax_excluded` added its tax under a `tax_included` company. The server writes the override whenever `price_include` is sent (`liwan_finance/controllers/finance_taxes.py:45-49`), and the web form always sends it (`apps/web/app/components/finance/records/recordForms.ts:77-82`).
- `_compute_price_include` does not look at a tax's use (`account_tax.py:322-329`). Of the Saudi purchase taxes, the 15%, reverse-charge, 0%, exempt and customs ones carry no override and would follow the setting; the withholding ones carry `tax_excluded`.
- The Saudi chart ships 17 sale taxes and none carries an override. Three are active: 15%, export of goods 0% and export of services 0%. The local zero-rated and exempt ones are inactive (`addons/l10n_sa/data/template/account.tax-sa.csv`). It ships no excise tax among them. Two fiscal positions apply themselves by the customer's country, and for a customer outside Saudi Arabia the export 0% replaces the 15% (`account.fiscal.position-sa.csv`, `account.tax-sa.csv`). The private healthcare and education 0% taxes need a buyer identified as a Saudi national (`addons/l10n_sa_edi/models/account_edi_format.py:276-284`).
- A ZATCA invoice refuses a line with no tax (`addons/l10n_sa_edi/models/account_edi_format.py:403-404`). So a product of a registered company that is not taxed needs a zero-rated or exempt tax, not an empty field.
- A new product takes its default sale tax from the session's companies, or from the root under stock's own elevation (`addons/account/models/product.py:44`). The chart loader has a hook after it loads a company's data (`addons/account/models/chart_template.py:700`).
- A ZATCA certificate names the parent's VAT number for a branch, and the invoice's QR code the branch's own (`addons/l10n_sa_edi/models/certificate.py:39,51,67`, `models/account_move.py:131`).

Stock product model (read in Odoo 19):

- `type` is `consu` (goods), `service` or `combo` (`addons/product/models/product_template.py:54-61`). `company_id` is optional (`:124`), and so is `categ_id` (`:81-86`); stock seeds three categories, Goods, Expenses and Services (`addons/product/data/product_data.xml:6-14`). With `sale`, a product cannot be given a company while another company has order lines for it, and clearing its company always works (`addons/sale/models/product_template.py:108-131`).
- Every internal user reads products, categories, attributes, tags and units. Create, write and unlink on all of them belong to `product.group_product_manager` (`addons/product/security/ir.model.access.csv`). The stock rule shows a product with no company or with a company at or above the user's (`security/product_security.xml:33-36`).
- `product.category` has no company. Its parent link deletes children with the parent (`models/product_category.py:21`). Tenant fields are `product_properties` on the template, defined per category in `product_properties_definition` (`product_template.py:186`, `product_category.py:27`). A read returns only the values the product's current category defines (`odoo/orm/fields_properties.py:200-214`). Inferred: values of the previous category stay stored and unreadable after a move, and a child category does not inherit its parent's fields.
- A variant's price is the template's `list_price` plus the extras of its attribute values, converted by unit and currency (`models/product_product.py:1101-1130`). `product.pricelist._get_product_price` is in `product` and accepts an empty pricelist (`models/product_pricelist.py:112-129`). Inferred: on an empty pricelist it returns the same number the sale line computes for an order with no pricelist rule.

LiwanOS rules (read):

- The company fence admits a record whose `company_id` is unset, and a record with a company only for a session that holds the capability in it (`liwan_core/models/ir_rule.py:50-53`). As with customers, the only shared state that works is no company.
- A rank-3 addon cannot depend on another rank-3 addon, and a rank-5 bridge cannot depend on another bridge (`tools/arch_policy.py:168-177`, strictly lower rank only). `liwan_payments` is rank 3, so it cannot read a `liwan_products` setting, and the reverse holds.
- A rank-3 name is not a commit scope; commits use `api`, `web` or an application area. A rank-3 addon has no standalone CI stage, and a bridge gets an `odoo-bridge` job.
- The bridge job accepts only a rank-5 addon whose manifest names exactly two LiwanOS modules, and then switches each side off in turn (`tools/app_isolation.py:79-83,100-109`, `deploy/ci/odoo-bridge.sh:14-26`). The tax bridge the spec names depends on one, `liwan_products`, and on stock `account`. `liwan_inbox_payments` and `liwan_appointments_payments` already name two, so neither can name `liwan_products`; they reach `liwan_customers` through their application side, which depends on it, and extend its service from there (`liwan_inbox/__manifest__.py`, `liwan_appointments/__manifest__.py`, `liwan_inbox_payments/models/customers_service.py`).
- Files that list installed addons and already name `liwan_customers`: `deploy/provision.sh`, `deploy/verification-checks.json`, `deploy/check_isolated.py`, `deploy/OPERATIONS.md`, `deploy/control-plane-outage/outage-fixture`, `tools/liwan_cli/operator.py`, `tools/test_module_closure.py`, `tools/shared_acceptance_live/up.sh`.
- The company profile has an extension hook (`liwan_api/serializers/company_profile.py:22,35,94`). It carries one text or selection field of the session's company in the identity or address section (`liwan_core/models/res_company.py:21-22,239-250`), so it cannot carry a choice that spans two fields, has a lock and belongs to the root.
- An addon adds its own capabilities to another addon's built-in role through `_liwan_builtin_role_grants` (`liwan_inbox_payments/models/access_service.py:42-46`). No application grants a shared service's capability to its own role today.

## 4. Design points the spec left open

The owner took the recommended option of every point that changes what a user sees or departs from the spec's wording (D4, D5, D9, D14, D18, D19), widened D5 and added D20, all on 2026-11-13. After the independent review the owner decided four more points that day: the default is "price includes tax" (D5), the tax choice is one decision for the whole establishment (D5, D25), vendor bills keep tax added (D5), and a shared product is managed from the root company only (D23). The other points are proposals the pull requests may still refine.

## 8. Risks

- The technical group gives product create and write at ACL level. Its rule limits it to service products; the reviewer checks that no other group its holders can have widens it, and that the `ir.http` guard still closes the generic endpoints.
- Under `establishment` sharing only root-company staff create and price products (D23). A tenant whose branches must manage their own chooses `branch`; the settings screen says so.

- The price function must equal the sale line's price. It is proved for the cases this phase has; pricelists reopen the proof.
- PR 1 removes the order service's tax code, a per-catalog choice and a payments setting in one step, and changes what a typed finance line means: under "price includes tax" an accountant's 115 is recorded as 100 and 15.
- The stock lock is permanent once an establishment has a journal item. A tenant that chose wrongly has no in-product way back; the default and the tax card are the only guards. Whether onboarding gets an explicit confirmation step is not decided.
- D25 changes `liwan_core`: every caller of the VAT helper now gets the root's answer. It gets its own tests and its own reviewer.
- D24 adds an elevated read. The reviewer checks that only ids, rates and amounts leave it.
- The payments migration raises on a development database whose branch chose another tax mode than its root, and the `upgrade` CI stage runs it on its fixture.

- The probe ran stock modules only. The same facts were not run with LiwanOS addons installed or through a ZATCA submission.
- Tax copies may sit on posted documents of development data, so they are archived and not deleted.
