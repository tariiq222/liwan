# Meetings
pages: meetings meeting

## Create a new meeting
page: /meetings/new
1. Open «Meetings» from the sidebar.
2. Click «New meeting» (may not show without permission).
3. Enter «Meeting title», «Date», «Start» and «End».
4. Add «Location» or «Meeting link» if needed.
5. Add attendees and the «Secretary», and write the «Agenda».
6. Click «Create meeting».

## Find a meeting
page: /meetings
1. Open «Meetings».
2. Type in «Search title, reference, or location».
3. Pick a state such as «Draft», «Confirmed» or «Completed».
4. Switch between «All meetings» and «My meetings».

## Confirm, complete or reopen a meeting
page: /meetings
1. Open the meeting.
2. While it is a draft click «Confirm» and confirm.
3. After it takes place click «Complete». The minutes are locked afterwards.
4. To reopen a completed or cancelled meeting click «Reopen».

## Write the minutes
page: /meetings
1. Open the meeting and go to the «Minutes» tab.
2. Click «Edit minutes».
3. Fill «Opening», add topics with «Add topic», then «Closing».
4. Click «Save changes».

## Add a recommendation and turn it into a task
page: /meetings
1. Open the meeting, tab «Recommendations».
2. Click «New recommendation».
3. Enter «Recommendation title», pick «Responsible» and a «Deadline», then click «Create recommendation».
4. To convert it, open «Convert recommendation to a task», pick the project and click «Convert to task».

## Print or download the minutes
page: /meetings
1. Open the meeting and click the actions button (three dots).
2. Choose «Preview and print» to print.
3. Or choose «Download minutes PDF» for the file.
