# Finance
pages: finance finance-invoice finance-bill finance-entry

## Create a sales invoice
page: /finance/sales/invoices/new
1. Open "Finance", then "Sales".
2. Click "New sales invoice".
3. Pick the customer in "Customer" (required).
4. Set "Date" and "Due date".
5. Under "Lines" fill "Description", "Quantity" and "Unit price"; click "Add line" for more. Tax is computed automatically unless you turn on "No tax" for a line.
6. Click "Create draft", then click "Post" on the invoice page.

## Create a vendor bill
page: /finance/purchases/bills/new
1. Open "Finance", then "Purchases".
2. Click "New vendor bill".
3. Pick the vendor in "Vendor" (required) and fill the date and lines.
4. Click "Create draft", then "Post" on the bill page.

## Register a payment on an invoice
page: /finance/payments/new
1. Open the posted invoice or bill.
2. Click "Register payment".
3. Check "Documents", then enter "Amount" (no more than the amount due) and "Payment date".
4. Choose the "Journal" and optionally a "Memo".
5. Click "Register payment".

## Cancel an invoice or reset it to draft
page: /finance/sales/invoices
1. Open the invoice.
2. Open the actions menu on the page.
3. Choose "Reset to draft" or "Cancel document" and confirm.
4. If payments exist, cancel them or reset them to draft first from "Payments".

## Add a customer or vendor
page: /finance/partners/new
1. Open "Finance", then "Customers and vendors".
2. Click "New partner".
3. Fill "Name" (required) and tick "Customer" and/or "Vendor" under "Type".
4. Add the tax number and address if you have them, then click "Create".

## Create a journal entry
page: /finance/accounting/entries/new
1. Open "Finance", then "Accounting", then click "New journal entry".
2. Choose the "Journal" and "Date".
3. In "Lines" pick an "Account" and enter either "Debit" or "Credit" per line. Add at least two lines with "Add line" so the totals match.
4. Click "Create draft", then "Post" on the entry page.

## View and export a report
page: /finance/reports/trial-balance
1. Open "Finance", then "Reports".
2. Pick the report in the side list, such as "Income statement" or "Balance sheet".
3. Set the dates with "From" and "To".
4. Click "Export CSV" or "Print".
