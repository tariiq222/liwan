# Projects and project tasks
pages: projects project task

## Create a new project
page: /projects/new
1. Open «Projects» from the sidebar.
2. Click «New project» (the button may not show without permission to create projects).
3. In the «Basics» step enter «Name» and choose «Type», «Manager» and «Sponsor».
4. Click «Next» to move through the steps. The steps depend on the project type.
5. To finish later click «Save as draft»: it creates the project and saves its file as a draft.
6. In the «Review» step check the data, then click «Create project».

## Find a project and filter the list
page: /projects
1. Open «Projects».
2. Type in «Search project names…».
3. Pick a «Status» and change «Sort by» if needed.
4. Switch between «Card view» and «Table view».
5. Click «Clear filters» to see the full list.

## Change a project's status
page: /projects
1. Open the project from «Projects».
2. Click the gear icon «Project settings» at the top (shown to people who can manage the project).
3. In the «Project status» section choose the new status.
4. When all tasks are done the status becomes «Done» automatically.

## Add a task to a project
page: /projects
1. Open the project, then the «Tasks» tab.
2. Click «New project task».
3. Enter «Task title» and check «Project».
4. When shown, pick «Milestone», «Planned start» and «Deadline».
5. Click «Create».

## Show project tasks on a board
page: /projects
1. Open the project, then the «Tasks» tab.
2. Choose «Board» instead of «List».
3. In «Group board by» pick «By status» or «By stage».
4. Drag a card to another column, or use «Move to…» in the card's task actions menu.

## Change a task's status
page: /tasks
1. Open the task.
2. Click the status badge at the top, such as «In progress».
3. Pick the new state, such as «Done» or «Cancelled». Only the states you are allowed appear.

## Add a milestone
page: /projects
1. Open the project, then «Milestones».
2. Click «New milestone» (project managers only).
3. Enter «Name», «Start date» and «Deadline» inside the project dates.
4. Click «Create».

## Archive a project
page: /projects
1. Open the project, then «Project settings».
2. In «Archive project» click «Archive project» and confirm.
3. To restore, choose «Archived» in the project list and click «Unarchive».
