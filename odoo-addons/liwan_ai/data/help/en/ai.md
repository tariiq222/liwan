# Liwan AI
pages: ai

## Ask Liwan AI
page: /ai
1. Click "Liwan AI" in the side rail.
2. Type in "Type your question or request…", or pick a starter question.
3. Click "Send".
4. Do not type health data, identity numbers or bank accounts.
5. The assistant only suggests and explains. It does not approve or send anything for you.

## Ask how to use LiwanOS
page: /ai
1. From the assistant panel on any page press «Ask about this page» to start a question about the page you are on.
2. Or open the «Liwan AI» page and press «Ask how to use LiwanOS».
3. Type your question, such as «How do I add a new employee?», or press one of the starter questions.
4. The assistant answers from the user guide and names the page that does the task.
5. This assistant does not read your company's data and changes nothing.

## Use the assistant from any page
page: /ai
1. Click the "Liwan AI" button at the top of the page to open the side panel.
2. Type your question and click "Send".
3. Click "New conversation" to start a new topic.
4. Use "Open full page" to enlarge it and "Close the assistant" to hide it.

## Approve an action the assistant proposes
page: /ai
1. A card titled "Needs your confirmation" summarizes the action.
2. Review it, then click "Run it" to approve or "Don't run" to refuse.
3. Nothing runs before you click "Run it".

## Review a draft the assistant prepared
page: /ai
1. A card titled "I prepared a draft" appears.
2. Click "Open the draft", review it on its real page and send it yourself.
3. Click "Discard the draft" if you do not need it.

## Run the setup assistant
page: /settings/apps/ai
1. Open "Settings", then "AI".
2. Click "Setup assistant".
3. Describe your business. The assistant proposes settings and applies nothing without your approval.

## Turn on the assistant for your company
page: /settings/apps/ai
1. Open "Settings", then "AI". This needs permission to manage assistant settings.
2. In "Use acknowledgement" tick the acceptance box and click "Sign the acknowledgement". The assistant does not run for anyone before that.
3. In "Provider" choose "Run on": "The LiwanOS provider" or "Your organization's own key", then click "Save changes".
