# Online payments
pages: payments

## Connect your payment gateway account and save its keys
page: /settings/apps/payments
1. Open «Settings», then «Online payments», and find «Payment gateways».
2. On the card of your gateway, find «Account keys».
3. Paste each key into its field. You find them in your account at the gateway.
4. Click «Save changes».
5. Saved keys are not shown again. Each one shows «Saved». Leave a field empty to keep the saved key.

## Turn the gateway on (test or live)
page: /settings/apps/payments
1. Open «Settings», then «Online payments».
2. Make sure every key is saved. Until then «Test» and «Live» cannot be chosen.
3. Under «Mode» choose «Test» to try the connection with no real money.
4. Choose «Live» for real payments, then click «Yes, turn on live payments».
5. Choose «Off» to stop taking payments. A mode change applies at once.

## Test the connection
page: /settings/apps/payments
1. Save the keys and turn on the «Test» or «Live» mode.
2. Click «Test connection» on the gateway card.
3. «The connection works» means the gateway accepted the keys.
