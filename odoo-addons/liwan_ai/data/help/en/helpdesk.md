# Support Desk
pages: helpdesk ticket

## Open a ticket for a customer
page: /helpdesk/new
1. Open «Support Desk» from the side rail, then «Tickets».
2. Click «New ticket».
3. Fill in «Subject» and «What is the request», which are required.
4. Choose the «Team» that receives the request, and the «Priority» if you want.
5. Choose «Category», «Type» and «Channel» when the company configures them.
6. Choose the «Customer» when the ticket is about one; leave it empty for an internal request from an employee.
7. Click «Open the ticket».

## Open and follow a request as an employee
page: /helpdesk/new
1. Open «Support Desk», then «Tickets».
2. Click «New ticket».
3. Write the «Subject» and «What is the request», choose the «Team», then click «Open the ticket».
4. The queue shows your own requests and your team's tickets.
5. Open your request by its number and answer in «Conversation».

## Search and filter the queue
page: /helpdesk
1. Open «Support Desk», then «Tickets».
2. Pick «Open», «Closed» or «All» in «Queue».
3. Type in «Search by ticket number, subject or requester» and press Enter.
4. Narrow with «Team», «Stage» and «Priority», and pick «Assigned to me».
5. Click a number to open the ticket; going back keeps your filters.

## Assign the ticket
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket from the queue.
2. Click «Assign to me», or pick another agent if you hold the assign capability.
3. Click «Unassign» to return it to the unassigned list.
4. The bell notifies whoever the ticket was assigned to.

## Move the ticket between stages
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket.
2. Choose the new stage from «Stage» at the top of the page.
3. The service level follows: reaching its target stage meets it.

## Close and reopen a ticket
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket and click «Close the ticket».
2. It moves to the closed stage and the closing date appears under «Details».
3. Click «Reopen» within the window the company policy allows.

## Answer the customer or leave an internal note
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket, then «Conversation».
2. Write a reply and click «Send»; the requester sees it.
3. Tick «Internal note, hidden from the requester» to write for the team only.
4. The first public reply records the first-response time.

## Attach a file
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket, then «Attachments».
2. Click «Attach a file» and choose it.
3. Click the name to download it, or the remove button to delete it.

## Merge duplicate tickets
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket you want to keep.
2. In «Merge duplicates», search by number or subject.
3. Tick the tickets to absorb, then click «Merge».
4. Their attachments and tags move, they close as duplicates, and the merge is written in the conversation.

## Send the ticket link to the customer
page: /helpdesk
1. Open the ticket, then «Customer page».
2. Click «Copy» and share the link with the customer only.
3. They open it without an account: they read the thread, reply and close the ticket.
4. The section is absent while the company turns the customer page off.

## Manage support teams
page: /settings/apps/helpdesk
1. Open «Settings», then «Applications», then «Support Desk».
2. In «Support teams» click «New team», write the name, and pick «Members» and «Team leader».
3. Click «Save». A team with open tickets, or the default team, cannot be archived.

## Manage stages and value lists
page: /settings/apps/helpdesk
1. Open the Support Desk settings.
2. In «Stages» add or edit a stage and set «Closed», «Waiting on the customer» and «Folded in the board».
3. In the lists add «Categories», «Tags», «Channels» and «Ticket types».
4. A stage holding open tickets cannot be archived.

## Manage service levels
page: /settings/apps/helpdesk
1. Open the Support Desk settings, then «Service levels».
2. Click «New service level», write the name, and set «Days» and «Hours».
3. Choose the «Target stage» that meets it.
4. Choose the «Teams», or leave it empty for every team, and the «Paused stages».
5. Click «Save»; the deadlines of the existing tickets follow at once.

## Configure the support policy
page: /settings/apps/helpdesk
1. Open the Support Desk settings.
2. Pick a «Preset» as a starting point.
3. Set «Who sees the tickets»: «Own tickets», «My teams» or «Whole company».
4. Set the «Default team», the «Default priority» and the «Ticket number prefix».
5. Set the reopen window, the first-response and resolution targets, «Customer page» and the auto-close days.
6. Click «Save the policy».

## Read the support reports
page: /helpdesk/reports
1. Open «Support Desk», then «Support reports».
2. Pick the «Period»: last 7, 30 or 90 days, or last year.
3. Read «First response» and «Resolution» against their targets, «Service levels» and «Open tickets».
4. «Agent workload» shows each agent's open, closed and overdue tickets, and «Tickets by stage» their distribution.
