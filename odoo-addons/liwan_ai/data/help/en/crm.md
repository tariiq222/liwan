# CRM (opportunities)
pages: crm opportunity

## Add a new opportunity
page: /crm/opportunities/new
1. Open «CRM» from the side rail, then «Opportunities».
2. Click «Add opportunity».
3. Fill in «Name» and «Phone», which are required. «Email» is optional.
4. Choose a «Source» if you want.
5. Leave «Agent» empty to take the record yourself. Someone with the assign capability can pick another agent or turn on «Put in the shared pool».
6. Click «Save» to open the new record.

## Find and filter opportunities
page: /crm/opportunities
1. Open «CRM», then «Opportunities».
2. Type in «Search by name, phone or email» and press Enter.
3. Narrow the list with «Stage», «Source» and «Status».
4. Turn on «Follow-up due» to see the ones whose call is due.
5. Click a name to open the record. Going back keeps your filters.
6. Choose «Stage board» to see one column per stage, and «List» to go back to the table.

## Record a call
page: /crm/opportunities
1. Open the opportunity from the list.
2. In the «Record a call» card choose the «Call outcome».
3. Add a «Note» and the «Duration (minutes)» if you want.
4. Click «Record the call».
5. The «Call log» shows the call, and the next call time appears when the outcome calls for a follow-up.

## Record a sale or close as lost
page: /crm/opportunities
1. Open the opportunity.
2. To sell, click «Sell a plan», choose the plan, adjust the «Final price» if there is a discount, then click «Confirm the sale».
3. To close it as lost, click «Close as lost» and choose the «Lost reason».
4. Click «Close as lost» again in the confirmation box.
5. Someone with the assign capability can reopen a lost record with «Reopen».

## Import opportunities from a file
page: /crm/imports/new
1. Open «CRM», then «Imports», and click «Import a file».
2. Choose a CSV or XLSX file up to 5 MB and 5,000 rows, with column headers in the first row.
3. Pick the column that holds each detail. Name, phone and source are required.
4. Click «Next» to see how many rows are New, Duplicate and Invalid.
5. Click «Import» with the number of new rows to finish. Duplicate rows are skipped.
