# Settings
pages: settings

## Edit company details
page: /settings/company
1. Open "Settings", then "Company".
2. In "Company identity" edit "Company name", "Commercial registration number" and "VAT number". The name is required.
3. Use "Choose a logo" to change the logo (PNG or JPEG, up to 2 MB).
4. Edit "National address" and "Contact details" in their cards.
5. Click "Save changes" in the card you edited.
6. "Country" cannot be changed. If you see "View only", you do not have permission to edit.

## Add a new user
page: /settings/team/users/new
1. Open "Settings", then "Team and permissions", then "Users".
2. Click "Create account". The button may be hidden without permission.
3. Choose "New account" and enter "Name" and "Login email", or choose "From employee" if shown.
4. Tick at least one "Role".
5. Enter "Password" (at least 8 characters) and "Confirm password".
6. Click "Create account".

## Change a user's role
page: /settings/team/users
1. Open "Settings", then "Team and permissions", then "Users".
2. Open the user and click the "Edit access" tab.
3. Tick or untick roles in "Assigned roles".
4. Review "Capability changes", then click "Save changes".

## Disable a user or reset a password
page: /settings/team/users
1. Open the user and click the "Account security" tab.
2. To reset, enter "Password" and "Confirm password", click "Reset password" and confirm.
3. To block sign-in, click "Disable access" and confirm. Use "Reactivate access" to restore it (a new password is required).

## Create a custom role
page: /settings/team/roles/new
1. Open "Settings", then "Team and permissions", then "Roles and permissions".
2. Click "Create role".
3. Enter "Arabic name" (required) and optionally "English name" and "Role purpose".
4. Tick the needed "Permissions", then click "Create role".

## Open an application's settings
page: /settings
1. Open "Settings".
2. Click the card of the application, such as "Projects", "Human Resources" or "Finance".
3. A card may be missing if you lack its permission.
