# Operations
pages: operations

## Create a new request
page: /operations/new
1. Open «Operations» and press «New request».
2. Enter the «Title» and choose the «Procedure» from the published procedures.
3. If the procedure needs a related record, choose it in «Related record».
4. Press «Create». The request is saved as a draft.

## Submit a request for approval
page: /operations
1. Open the draft from «My requests».
2. Press «Submit for approval» in the «Next step» card.
3. Confirm in the message that appears.
4. The «Approval stages» then start in order.

## Approve or reject a request
page: /operations/pending
1. Open «Awaiting my review» and open the request.
2. In «Your decision is needed» choose «Approve», «Reject» or «Return for changes».
3. Write «Comment or decision reason». Reject and return need a comment.
4. Confirm the decision.

## Create an approval procedure
page: /settings/apps/operations/processes
1. Open Settings, then the «Operations» application settings, then «Work procedures».
2. Press «New procedure» and enter the «Title» and «Approval outcome».
3. Under «Approval stages» enter the «Stage name» and choose the «Reviewers». Use «Add stage» for more.
4. Press «Create», open the procedure and press «Publish».
